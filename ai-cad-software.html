Questions

Does DX-CAD send my models to an AI service?

Only if you use the assistant with a provider your company allows, and only what the policy lets it send. With AI switched off by policy, DX-CAD makes no connection to any AI service.

Which AI providers can I use?

Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini and Azure OpenAI with your own key, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a model on your own servers.

Can IT turn AI off?

Yes, for the whole company, by Group Policy or a policy file, or at install with /NOAI. Users cannot turn it back on.

Can it make a part from a sentence?

Yes, through the assistant or an agent such as Claude Desktop. For a quick STEP file from a description with no CAD open, see Designmatix Text-to-CAD.
